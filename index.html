About

Products built to be kept, not flipped

A lot of online products launch quickly and fade just as fast. Critter works the other way. We start or acquire businesses, give them the team and infrastructure they need, and keep running them for years.

  • Built in-house

    Product, design, engineering and support are handled by our own team, not handed off to whoever is cheapest.

  • Independent brands

    Every company keeps its own name, product and community. Critter stays in the background and does the heavy lifting.

  • Run for the long term

    We don't build to sell. Products keep getting updates, fixes and support long after launch day.

Standards

What Critter has to offer . . .

  • Quality products

  • Support that answers
