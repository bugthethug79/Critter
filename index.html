01What we do

We build and run online brands.

Critter is the parent company behind a growing set of online businesses. Each one has its own name, its own product and its own people. We give them the same standards and the same backing.

  1. 01

    Build

    We start companies from a blank page: name, product, support, the lot. Every niche gets a proper effort, however small.

  2. 02

    Run

    We don't launch and leave. Updates ship, tickets get answered, and the same people are still there a year later.

  3. 03

    Grow

    When a good business needs a steady home, we bring it in and look after it the same way we look after our own.

02How we work

Quiet, careful, and around for good.

01020304 / 04
  1. 01

    Made well, whatever it is.

    A small utility gets the same attention as a full platform. The size of the niche doesn't change the standard.

  2. 02

    We stay.

    No disappearing sellers, no abandoned products, no silent inboxes. If something breaks, we fix it and we tell you when.

  3. 03

    Community first.

    Every brand we run is built around the people who use it. They shape what comes next; we do the work.

  4. 04

    Organized, on purpose.

    We move at a deliberate pace. Fewer launches, each one finished properly, with support ready on day one.

A bug is a critter. We started with one small tool and a community that kept recommending it. That's still the model, just across more niches.
